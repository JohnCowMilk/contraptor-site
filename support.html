Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Building, tutorials, and browsing/playing the Community all work signed‑out. You sign in with iCloud only to post, rate, follow, claim a username, or appear on the leaderboard.
  • How do I report or block someone? Tap Report on any Community challenge or solution, or block a builder to hide their content. Reports go straight to us for review.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete Account. This removes your public content and releases your username.
  • Something isn’t syncing. Make sure you’re signed into iCloud and connected; relaunching the app often resolves a temporary sync lag.
